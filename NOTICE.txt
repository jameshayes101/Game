Grid Wars 2 - Miyoo Flip port
------------------------------
Game: Grid Wars 2 by Mark Incitti (2006). Music by "Swith" (see GridWars-Readme.txt).
Source used: https://github.com/mmatyas/GridWars  (fork aimed at embedded devices; listed as GPL in
distribution packaging - check the upstream repository for the authoritative license text before
redistributing this package).
gl4es by Sebastien Chevalier (ptitSeb): https://github.com/ptitSeb/gl4es  (MIT license).
Music in music/*.wav is rendered from the original .it tracker songs shipped with the game.
